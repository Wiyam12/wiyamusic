Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WiyaMusic (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the WiyaMusic mobile application and related website. WiyaMusic is a free music streaming app. We designed it to keep as much data as possible on your device.

1. Summary

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show ads or require a subscription.
  • Most library data (playlists, likes, offline songs, settings) stays on your device.
  • Streaming and search go through third-party content sources; those services have their own privacy policies.

2. Information we process

2.1 Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, WiyaMusic may store locally:

  • Playlists, liked songs, recently played history
  • App settings and preferences (theme, language, audio quality)
  • Offline audio files and artwork you choose to download
  • Optional listening statistics / “Time Machine” style recap data (if enabled)
  • Backup files you export or import yourself

This information remains on your device unless you back it up, share it, or clear it from the App.

2.2 Device permissions

The App may request permissions only when needed for a feature:

  • Photo library / camera — to choose or capture an image for custom playlist artwork (via the system / photo-picker libraries).
  • Notifications — to show download progress while saving songs offline.
  • Network access — to stream music, search, fetch lyrics/artwork, and check for app updates.
  • Storage / files — to save offline music and backup/restore data.
  • Background audio — to keep playback running when the App is in the background.

Some photo-picker libraries may also declare location-related APIs even when WiyaMusic itself does not use your location for streaming. You can deny any permission you do not want to grant; related features may be limited.

2.3 Network activity and third parties

When you search, stream, download artwork/lyrics, or check for updates, the App communicates with external services (for example content providers and our update/version endpoint on this website). Those providers may receive technical information such as IP address and request metadata under their own policies. WiyaMusic does not host, own, or distribute copyrighted audio files.

2.4 App updates

On supported platforms, WiyaMusic may check this website for a newer version and show an optional update prompt. That request typically includes only standard network metadata needed to fetch the version file — not your library or listening history.

3. How we use information

Information processed by the App is used to:

  • Provide streaming, search, lyrics, and offline playback
  • Remember your library and preferences on the device
  • Show download progress notifications
  • Let you customize playlist artwork and share song cards
  • Offer optional update notices

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may leave your device when:

  • You stream or search using third-party content services
  • You export a backup or share content using the system share sheet
  • You choose to install an update from our website

5. Data retention

Local data remains until you delete it in the App, clear app storage, uninstall the App, or overwrite it via backup restore. We do not operate a separate user account database for WiyaMusic library data.

6. Children’s privacy

WiyaMusic is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). Do not use the App if you are under that age.

7. Your choices

  • Revoke permissions in your device system settings
  • Clear library data, downloads, or listening stats in the App
  • Use offline mode where available to limit network features
  • Decline optional update prompts
  • Uninstall the App to remove local App data

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or WiyaMusic can be sent via the project issue tracker: github.com/Wiyam12/wiyamusic/issues.

10. Disclaimer

WiyaMusic and its contributors do not host, own, or distribute any copyrighted audio content. The App provides access to content through plugins and external sources. All trademarks, songs, audio files, and related content remain the property of their respective owners. Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their use of the App complies with local laws, copyright regulations, and the terms of service of the respective content providers.
